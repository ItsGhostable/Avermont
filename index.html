Questions

What owners usually ask.

First replies typically arrive in the first 7–10 days of sending. Qualified calls on your calendar usually start in weeks 3–4, with volume ramping through week 6 as we optimize. We set expectations together at kickoff based on your market.

We focus on B2B companies with a clear buyer and a sales process worth filling — typically SaaS, agencies, professional services, and B2B product companies doing $500K–50M in revenue. If your customer is another business and your deal size justifies outbound, we should talk.

We guarantee activity, transparency, and rigor — every email sent, every reply, every booked call, reported weekly. Revenue depends on your offer, pricing, and sales process, which is why we audit those first. If the math doesn't work, we'll tell you on the call.
